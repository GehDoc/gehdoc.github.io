Featured Project: SVG to Video

SVG to Video is a professional converter for turning animated vector graphics (CSS & SMIL animations) into MP4, WebM, MKV, MOV, aPNG, and GIF formats. Features frame-accurate rendering, CLI automation, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server integration.
